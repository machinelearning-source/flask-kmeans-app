Part 1: Manual K-Means Simulation

Step-by-step mathematical tracing of the clustering process over 3 iterations.

Dataset Context

We analyze a sample of {{ n_rows }} flight records with two numerical variables:

  • Distance (km): the geographic distance traveled by the flight.
  • Duration (min): the total time of the flight in minutes.

The objective is to identify natural groups of flights (e.g., short-haul, medium-haul, and long-haul) using K-Means with three clusters.

Variance (WCSS) Comparison

The Within-Cluster Sum of Squares decreases in every iteration, proving that the algorithm is converging: the records are getting closer to their cluster centers and the groups are becoming more homogeneous.

Final Interpretation

After 3 iterations the centroids stabilize. Cluster 1 groups short-haul flights (about 392 km, ~50 min), Cluster 2 groups medium-haul flights (about 1,499 km, ~141 min), and Cluster 3 groups long-haul flights (about 2,613 km, ~236 min).
